PROJECT OBJECTIVE
Analyze customer acquisition cohorts, repeat-purchase behavior, retention patterns, RFM characteristics, customer segments, and business-level retention insights.

RETENTION DEFINITION
For Part 1, a customer is considered a repeat/retained customer when the customer has at least one additional order beyond the first observed order. Cohort retention is calculated by tracking unique customers purchasing during each month relative to their cohort month.
